# Roadmap / wishlist

Ideas collected for later (2026-10-20), roughly in the order they were asked for. Notes are first thoughts, not decisions.

- [ ] **Twinkly integration** – control Twinkly lights next to WLED, possibly through Home Assistant's Twinkly integration (or Twinkly's local API directly, so they can join rooms, groups and the grid).
- [ ] **Play video files across a grid** – decode a video (e.g. with ffmpeg in the add-on), sample each frame at the mapped LED positions of a grid and stream it like a hub effect.
- [ ] **Music with beat sync / auto show** (e.g. Christmas music) – play or listen to music, detect beats and energy, and drive effects from it automatically. Could reuse WLED's sound-reactive data or analyse audio in the hub.
- [ ] **Schedules / calendar** – time-based control: turn rooms, effects, custom effects and scenes on and off at set times or at sunset/sunrise (could also lean on Home Assistant automations).
- [ ] **Groups expanded by default in the browser** – on wide screens show groups unfolded; keep the current compact layout on phones.
- [ ] **Android app with remote access** – reach the hub when away from home, via Home Assistant (Nabu Casa / ingress) or another secure tunnel, without being on the local network.
- [ ] **Online/offline for every WLED device on the home screen** – a clear status for each device on the Rooms page.
- [ ] **Edit the layout of the Rooms page** – reorder and arrange room cards.
- [ ] **Small grid preview on every room card** – a mini live preview of the room's LEDs on its card (the room sheet already has one).
